A note on EU withdrawal rights

Under EU rules on digital content, the statutory 14-day withdrawal right ends once delivery of digital content begins with your consent (which happens the moment your purchase unlocks the download). Our voluntary guarantee above is broader than the statutory right — we honor the full 14 days regardless. Your other statutory consumer rights are unaffected.
