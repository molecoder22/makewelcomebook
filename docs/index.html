Questions hosts ask us

Do I need any design skills or software?

No. You type answers into a form; the book designs itself. There is nothing to install and no Canva account needed.

How do I get the PDF?

Click “Download PDF” — your browser's print dialog opens and you choose “Save as PDF” (or print it directly). A4 pages, ready for any printer or print shop.

Can I update it later?

Yes, forever and free. Your answers are saved in your browser — change the WiFi password, add a new café, reprint in a minute.

Does it work for VRBO / Booking.com rentals?

Yes — the book is yours and brand-neutral. It works for any short-term rental, guesthouse, or even a guest room at home.
