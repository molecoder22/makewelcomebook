# Can Make Welcome Book make €1,000 profit per month?
**Written 2026-09-02, in answer to Mário's direct question. No spin.**

## Short answer

**Possible — but not from where we are today, and not from Google Ads.** It needs Etsy to
mature into a real shop with reviews, plus at least two more traffic sources, plus a higher
average order value. Realistically that is a **3–6 month build**, and it is not guaranteed.
If I had to put a number on it: **roughly a 1-in-4 chance of reaching €1,000/month within
six months** if the work is done consistently. A more likely good outcome in that window is
**€300–600/month**.

## What €1,000/month actually means in units

Profit per sale, after payment and marketplace fees:

| Channel | Price seen by buyer | Net to us |
|---|---|---|
| Own site (Stripe) | $29 | ≈ €23.36 |
| Etsy, full price | $29 | ≈ €21 |
| Etsy, during the 30 % sale | $20.30 | ≈ €15 |

So €1,000 **profit** per month means, depending on channel mix:

- **43 sales/month** if everything sold at full price on our own site, with zero ad spend
- **50 sales/month** at a realistic blended €20 net
- **68 sales/month** if most sales come through Etsy at the discounted price

Call it **~50 sales a month, or 1.7 sales a day, every day.** Today we have **2 real sales
in ten days** (a third Stripe payment was Mário's own test). We are at roughly 4 % of that
rate.

## Where 50 sales a month could come from

| Channel | Realistic monthly sales | Confidence | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search Ads | 5–8 | High — measured | Now (capped, see ADS-AUDIT) |
| Etsy | 15–40 | Medium | 3–6 months, needs reviews |
| Own-site SEO (articles + WiFi tool) | 3–10 | Low–medium | 4–6 months |
| Microsoft/Bing Ads | 2–4 | Medium | Needs Mário to open an account |
| Higher-value tier ($49 multi-property) | reduces the target by ~20 % | Medium | 2–4 weeks of work |
| Pinterest | 0 (shelved by Mário) | — | — |

Add the optimistic ends together and you get about 50–60. Add the pessimistic ends and you
get about 25. That spread *is* the honest answer: the plan is arithmetically possible, and
every single line has to go well.

## The four things that would actually have to happen

1. **Etsy has to become the engine, and that runs on reviews.** The shops beating us in this
   niche have 4,600–8,000 reviews. Etsy's algorithm rewards sales velocity and review count,
   both of which compound. The first 10 reviews are the hard part — after each sale, message
   the buyer politely and ask. This is the single highest-leverage activity in the whole
   venture, and it cannot be rushed or bought.
2. **Average order value has to rise.** At €15–23 net per sale, 50 sales is a grind. A $49
   multi-property or "host bundle" tier — same product, more properties, maybe the WiFi sign
   pack — cuts the required number of sales by a fifth or more for a few days of work. This is
   the cheapest lever available.
3. **Organic traffic has to start.** The free WiFi-QR-sign generator is already live and is
   the right kind of asset: it ranks for a real search term ("airbnb wifi sign", 50/mo, plus
   variants), costs nothing per visitor, and puts our builder one click away. It needs
   supporting articles to compound. Start once sale #3 lands.
4. **Google Ads has to stay small and profitable, not grow.** See ADS-AUDIT-2026-09-02.md:
   the entire relevant US search market is ~1,300 searches/month and shrinking 45–65 % YoY on
   the head terms. That channel maxes out around 6–8 sales/month. Pushing more money into it
   destroys the profit rather than scaling it.

## What would make me say "stop"

- Etsy at 60 days with heavy views but near-zero sales → the listing or the price is wrong,
  not the traffic.
- Etsy at 60 days with almost no views → Etsy SEO has not taken; the new-listing boost is
  over and the shop did not earn its ranking.
- Google Ads spending another €40 post-cleanup with 0 sales → pause it.
- Total spend approaching the €250 budget with under €150 recovered → the venture has told
  us its answer and the remaining money is better kept.

## ⚠️ Legal / tax warning — read this before scaling

Mário operates **without a živnosť up to €2,200/year** in revenue.

- €1,000 **profit** per month implies roughly €13,000–15,000 of **revenue** per year.
- That is **six times** over the no-živnosť ceiling.
- At a €1,000/month run rate, the €2,200 ceiling is crossed in about **ten weeks**.

Current position (2026-09-02): **≈ €49 of real revenue** (2 genuine Stripe sales; the third
payment was a self-test). Etsy: €0 so far. There is plenty of headroom right now — but the
živnosť question has to be settled with an accountant **before** any serious scaling, not
after the money arrives. It is the one blocker that cannot be solved with more work on the
product.

## Bottom line

The product is good, the funnel converts (8.3 % on ads, well above typical), and the unit
economics work. What is missing is *volume*, and volume in this niche lives on Etsy and in
organic search, not in Google Ads. €1,000/month is a realistic 6-month **target**, not a
2-month **expectation** — and the honest base case is a few hundred euros a month of profit,
which for a €250 investment would already be a good outcome.
